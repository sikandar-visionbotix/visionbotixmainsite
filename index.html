About Visionbotix

A research-driven engineering company

Visionbotix is a software development and cybersecurity company headquartered in Islamabad, Pakistan, serving clients worldwide. We are programmers, machine learning engineers, hardware designers and security researchers developing state-of-the-art algorithms and systems — we don't just build what's asked, we work with our clients to shape ideas into industry-standard, competitive products.

That same discipline drives our own products: encryption designed in silicon, security platforms built for the most restricted networks, and automation systems running in the field every day.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Visionbotix do?

Visionbotix is a software development and cybersecurity company. We build custom software, web and mobile applications, AI and machine learning systems, AI agents, computer vision solutions, IoT, robotics and embedded/FPGA products — and we develop our own cybersecurity products: the VX-1 hardware IP encryptor, the ThreatIQ internal defense platform for air-gapped networks, and a Spirent-based Cyber Range.

Does Visionbotix build AI agents and custom AI solutions?

Yes. We develop custom AI end to end — machine learning and deep learning models, AI agents and LLM-powered assistants, chatbots, intelligent workflow automation, and computer vision systems — deployed on cloud or edge devices.

What cybersecurity products does Visionbotix offer?

Three: the VX-1 IP Encryptor, an FPGA-based plug-and-play hardware encryptor with AES-256 at line rate; ThreatIQ, an internal defense layer for air-gapped networks combining vulnerability assessment, user behavior analytics and compliance benchmarking; and the Cyber Range, a Spirent-based platform for red/blue team exercises, device benchmarking and attack simulation.

Can Visionbotix develop mobile and web applications?

Yes. We build native and cross-platform Android and iOS mobile apps, and fast, scalable web platforms and cloud backends — designed, developed, tested and supported as complete products.

Which industries does Visionbotix serve?

Defense, government, financial institutions, critical infrastructure, telecom and ISPs, data centers, agriculture (smart livestock farming) and general enterprise — worldwide.
