A gym runs on relationships between members and trainers, but the admin around them was scattered. We pulled
memberships, schedules, plans and payments into one platform and gave each role its own view.

Members use the mobile app to see their plan, chat with their trainer and pay. Trainers upload workouts and build
nutrition plans from a dashboard, and the system handles renewal reminders so front-desk staff don't have to.
