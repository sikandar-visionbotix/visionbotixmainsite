Writing for search involves several tools before a single word is published. We folded them into one workflow: pick a
topic, choose keywords from the planner, let the GPT-4 pipeline draft the article, and run an SEO audit on the result.

When the draft is ready it exports as HTML or Markdown, or goes straight to a WordPress site, so the content team edits
rather than assembles.
