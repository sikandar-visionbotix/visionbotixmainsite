"Can you send the draft by Thursday?" is a task, but it usually stays a chat message. The assistant reads messages
like that, creates the task with an owner and a due date, and estimates its priority from the conversation.

It then does the follow-up work: standups and reminders in Slack, a sync with Notion, and a dashboard that shows how
work is moving across the team.
