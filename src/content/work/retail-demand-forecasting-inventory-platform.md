Ordering on instinct works until the season changes. We trained time-series models on the retailer's own sales data so
each product's forecast reflects its seasonal pattern.

The forecasts drive a depletion simulation that predicts when each line will run low and raises a reorder alert in
time. Buyers see it all by category and can export the figures straight into their purchasing process.
