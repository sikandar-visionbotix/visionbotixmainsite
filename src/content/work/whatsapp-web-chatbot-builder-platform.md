Most small businesses want the same few bots: answer common questions, collect a lead, open a support ticket. We
turned those into building blocks in a visual flow editor, with Dialogflow handling the language understanding
behind each step.

A finished flow goes live on WhatsApp through Twilio or as a chat widget on the customer's site. Conversation analytics
show how each bot is being used, and import and export make it simple to copy a working bot to a new account.
