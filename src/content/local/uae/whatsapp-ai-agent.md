faq:
  - q: Can it reply in Gulf Arabic?
    a: Yes. It replies in Arabic when the customer writes in Arabic, including Gulf dialect, and in English when they write in English. We set the Arabic to Gulf phrasing or Modern Standard Arabic as you prefer, test it on real messages from your chats and review the replies with your team before launch. Answers come only from your catalog and policies.
  - q: Can customers pay with Tabby or Tamara in the chat?
    a: Not inside WhatsApp itself. The agent sends a link to your store's checkout, where customers choose Tabby, Tamara, a card or cash on delivery, as your store allows. Payment details never pass through the agent; it only sees from the order in your store whether it has been paid.
  - q: Where is the data stored?
    a: Chat history, logs and order lookups can be hosted in a UAE cloud region, or in the EU or US if you prefer. Messages pass through Meta's WhatsApp servers on the way, which your privacy notice should cover. Clinic chats with patient details stay in a UAE region.
  - q: We are in the DIFC or ADGM. What changes?
    a: The data protection law changes. DIFC firms follow the DIFC law and its Regulation 10, which asks for clear notice when someone first uses an AI system, so the agent opens each chat by saying it is an AI assistant. ADGM firms follow the ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021, and onshore businesses the federal PDPL.
  - q: Can it send offers to all our WhatsApp contacts?
    a: Only to people who opted in to hear from you. Offers go out as Meta-approved marketing templates with an easy opt-out, and anyone who opts out is not messaged again. Messaging people who did not ask can hurt your number's quality rating, so we start with recent buyers and enquirers.
  - q: Does it keep working in Ramadan and on Eid?
    a: Yes. The agent runs every day, including Fridays, weekends, Ramadan nights and Eid. We switch it to your Ramadan hours and delivery cut-offs, add iftar and suhoor timings for restaurants, and chats that need a person wait in your inbox until your team is back.
  - q: Does the agent phone customers?
    a: No. It works on WhatsApp and Instagram messages only. When a lead asks for a call, it books one of your team's call slots, and we set those between 09:00 and 18:00, the hours the UAE telemarketing rules allow for marketing calls.
