faq:
  - q: Can I keep my existing number, including a 1300 or 1800 number?
    a: Yes. You keep your number and forward calls to the agent through your phone provider, either all calls, only after hours or only when your lines are busy. A 1300 or 1800 number forwards the same way as a landline or mobile. If you want a separate line, we set up a new local number.
  - q: Does it work with Dental4Windows or Core Practice?
    a: Possibly, but we check first. Access for outside booking tools is arranged through the vendor, so in the first week we confirm with the vendor whether a connection can be switched on for your practice. If it cannot, the agent takes the booking request with all the details and your team confirms the slot.
  - q: What does the agent do if someone calls with an emergency?
    a: For a life-threatening emergency, it tells the caller to hang up and call triple zero (000) and alerts your team straight away. For urgent but non-life-threatening calls, such as dental pain or a burst pipe, it follows your rules and either transfers the call, texts the on-call person or books the next urgent slot. It never gives medical advice.
  - q: Is it legal to record calls in every state?
    a: Recording rules come from state and territory law as well as Commonwealth law, and they differ. That is why recording is off by default. When you turn it on, the greeting tells every caller the call is recorded, and recordings stay in your Australian account under your retention rules. Your own legal adviser can confirm the position for your state.
  - q: Can it answer bulk-billing and health fund questions?
    a: Yes, from the answers you approve, such as whether you bulk bill, which funds you are a preferred provider for and whether a gap applies. It does not quote a patient's own fees or process claims; those questions go to your front desk.
  - q: Will tradies get the job details they need?
    a: Yes. The agent asks for the address, the problem, access details and a contact number, creates the job in ServiceM8 or simPRO, and texts urgent callouts to the on-call mobile. Quotes and pricing questions beyond your approved list go to you.
  - q: Where is the call data stored?
    a: In an Australian cloud region, encrypted in transit and at rest, and kept out of model training. Transcripts follow your retention rules and access is role-based, so only the staff you choose can read them. Your privacy officer gets a data-flow document before go-live, which also shows where calls are processed.
