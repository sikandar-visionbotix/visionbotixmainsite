rules:
  - name: UK GDPR Article 13, telling callers
    body: When personal data is collected from someone, the controller must tell them at that time who it is, why the data is used, on what legal basis and how long it is kept. The greeting says the caller is speaking to an AI assistant and whether the call is recorded, and points to your privacy notice.
    source: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/article/13
  - name: Recording business calls (2018 Regulations)
    body: The 2018 interception regulations let a business record calls relevant to its business for purposes such as establishing facts or checking standards, provided it has made all reasonable efforts to tell everyone who may use the line that calls may be recorded. When you turn recording on, the greeting says so on every call.
    source: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/356/regulation/4
  - name: PECR regulation 19, automated marketing calls
    body: Direct marketing calls that play recorded matter through an automated calling system need the subscriber's prior consent. The receptionist answers inbound calls and makes no marketing calls; any outbound calls you add go only to people who asked for them.
    source: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2426/regulation/19
  - name: PECR regulation 22, marketing texts
    body: Marketing by electronic mail, which includes text messages, needs consent unless the soft opt-in applies (an existing customer, similar products and a free way to refuse every time). Booking confirmations and reminders carry no promotions, and any marketing texts you add check consent first.
    source: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2426/regulation/22

faq:
  - q: Does it work with a UK 0333 number?
    a: Yes. You keep your 0333 number and your provider forwards calls to the agent, all the time, after hours or only when your lines are busy. The same works for local 01 and 02 numbers, 0800 freephone numbers and mobiles, and callers still dial the number they know.
  - q: Do callers have to be told the call is recorded?
    a: Yes, and the greeting does it. When you turn recording on, every caller hears that the call is recorded and that they are speaking to an AI assistant before the conversation starts, with a pointer to your privacy notice. Your data protection lead confirms the wording.
  - q: Can it handle practices in England and Scotland with different bank holidays?
    a: Yes. Each site gets its own opening hours, bank holiday calendar and diary, so a Scottish branch can close on St Andrew's Day while an English branch stays open. Callers hear the hours for the site they rang.
  - q: Can it tell NHS patients from private patients?
    a: Yes, if you want it to. We set it up to ask whether the caller is an NHS or private patient, offer slots from the matching appointment types and answer from the policies you approve. Questions about NHS eligibility or exemptions go to your team.
  - q: Can it send appointment reminders by text?
    a: Yes. Confirmations and reminders go out by text or WhatsApp with no promotional content. If you want marketing texts too, they go only to people who gave consent or fall under the soft opt-in, and every one offers a free way to opt out.
  - q: Does it understand strong regional accents?
    a: We test it with sample calls in the accents your callers use before go-live and tune it in the first weeks. If it still cannot understand a caller after a second try, it offers a person or a call back rather than guessing.
  - q: Is our callers' data kept in the UK?
    a: Yes, stored data is. Call recordings, transcripts and summaries are kept in a UK cloud region, we sign processor terms with you under Article 28 of the UK GDPR and your retention rules delete them automatically. Where your policy requires it, we pick voice and model providers that process calls in the UK or EU.
