## Prompt-first search vs a classic directory

| Question | Classic lawyer directory | Wakeel (prompt-first) |
|---|---|---|
| First step | Pick a category and a city from menus | Describe the problem in your own words |
| Language | Usually English legal terms | Urdu, English or Roman Urdu, typed or spoken |
| What the system understands | The filters you chose | City, case type, court level and budget, from your description |
| Results | A long list of names | A short list of verified advocates, each with a reason it fits |
| Who it works for | People who already know what they need | Anyone with a problem, including first-time users |
