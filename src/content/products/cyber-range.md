## Cyber range exercises vs classroom training

| Compared on | Classroom training | Cyber range exercises |
|---|---|---|
| Environment | Slides, labs and theory | Production-grade infrastructure on realistic traffic |
| Attacks | Described or demonstrated | Launched live by a red team |
| Defense practice | Discussed in class | Blue team detects, isolates and responds in real time |
| Device knowledge | Vendor datasheets | Devices benchmarked to RFC 2544, RFC 2889 and RFC 3511 |
| Risk to real systems | None, but little realism | None, with full realism |
| Review afterwards | Quizzes and notes | Attack callflows and detailed reports |
