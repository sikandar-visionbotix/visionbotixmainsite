## Build vs buy vs partner

| Compared on | Build in-house | Buy an AI tool | Partner with a studio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best for | Teams with AI engineers and time to learn | Common tasks a tool already does well | Work tied to your systems, data or product |
| Time to first result | Months, while the team learns | Days to weeks | Weeks, with a demo every week |
| Fit to your process | Full, once built | You adapt to the tool | Built around your process |
| Who owns the result | You | The vendor | You, 100% of the code and IP |
| Ongoing effort | Your team maintains everything | Vendor updates, your admin | Your team, or an optional monthly plan |
| Main risk | Slow progress and stalled pilots | Data terms and lock-in | Unclear scope, so insist on a fixed one |
