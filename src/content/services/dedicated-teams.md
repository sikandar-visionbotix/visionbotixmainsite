| Scaling up or down | Easy but inconsistent | Slow and costly | Agreed in the contract |
| AI and specialist skills | Hit and miss | Hard to find | Available across the team |
| Continuity when someone leaves | Knowledge leaves with them | Rehire from scratch | Handled by the lead and the team |
