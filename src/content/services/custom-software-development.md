faq:
  - q: What is custom software development?
    a: It is software designed and built for your own processes, users and data, instead of a product you rent and adapt to. You decide what it does, it connects to the systems you already use, and you own the code outright.
  - q: Should we buy off-the-shelf software or build custom?
    a: Buy when a mainstream product fits most of your process and the gap is minor. Build when the gap is the part that makes your business different, when you are paying for several tools to fake one workflow, or when the tool cannot connect to your other systems. Often the right answer is both, with a custom layer on top of what you already own.
  - q: Can you take over a codebase another team built?
    a: Yes. We start with a short audit of the code, infrastructure and documentation, tell you plainly what is sound and what is risky, and then fix the urgent issues and keep shipping. You get a written report either way.
  - q: How do you modernize a legacy system without stopping the business?
    a: We replace it in slices. The new system runs alongside the old one, data syncs between them, and each module moves over only after it is tested against real records. Your team keeps working throughout, and there is always a way back until cutover.
  - q: Can you integrate with our ERP, CRM or accounting software?
    a: Usually, yes. If the system has an API, webhooks or a database we can reach, we can integrate with it. We check access and limits during discovery, before you commit to a fixed price.
  - q: How long does a custom software project take?
    a: A focused first release, such as an internal tool or a portal, is usually live in 8–14 weeks. Larger systems are planned in milestones that each deliver something your team can use, and you see a working demo every week.
  - q: Do we own the software and the code?
    a: Yes. The contract assigns 100% of the code and IP to you on payment, the repository lives in your account, and the documentation is written so any competent team can maintain it, with or without us.

## Off-the-shelf SaaS vs no-code vs custom software

| Compared on | Off-the-shelf SaaS | No-code platform | Custom software |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best for | Standard processes many companies share | Simple internal apps and quick experiments | Workflows that make your business different |
| Fits your exact process | Partly, you adapt to it | Up to the platform's limits | Yes, built around it |
| Connects to your other systems | Through its own integrations | Popular apps only | Any system with an API or database |
| Scales with users and data | Yes, at per-seat cost | Often hits limits | Yes, designed for your volumes |
| Time to start | Days | Days to weeks | 8–14 weeks for a first release |
| Who owns the code | The vendor | The platform | You own all of it |
