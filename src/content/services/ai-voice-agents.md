faq:
  - q: What is an AI voice agent?
    a: It is software that answers or makes phone calls and talks like a person. Speech recognition turns the caller's words into text, a language model decides what to say and do, and a synthetic voice replies, while connections to your calendar and CRM let it book, look up and update records during the call.
  - q: Do callers have to be told they are talking to an AI?
    a: We build the disclosure in for every client, whatever the local rules say, because callers trust a clear answer more than a surprise. The greeting says it is an AI assistant, and asking for a person transfers the call or books a callback.
  - q: Can it make outbound calls?
    a: Yes, for reminders, confirmations, lead follow-up and qualification. In many countries, including the US and UK, automated or AI-voiced calls need prior consent and must respect calling hours and do-not-call lists. We build consent checks, calling windows and opt-outs into every campaign, but your legal team should confirm the rules for each market you call.
  - q: How natural does it sound?
    a: Close to a person on a good line. We pick a voice from ElevenLabs or another provider, tune how quickly it responds and let it handle interruptions and pauses. You hear recorded test calls before go-live and can change the voice or wording at any point.
  - q: Can it work with our existing phone system?
    a: Yes. You can forward your current number, use a new Twilio number or connect a SIP trunk to your PBX or contact center platform. The agent can transfer calls to extensions, queues or mobile numbers based on your rules.
  - q: Can it handle callers in different languages?
    a: Yes. The agent greets callers in your main language and switches when the caller does, across English, Arabic, Urdu and other major languages. We choose a voice per language and test each one with the phrasing and accents your callers actually use.
  - q: Are call recordings stored safely?
    a: Recording is optional and announced when it is on. Recordings and transcripts are encrypted, stored in the region you choose and deleted on your schedule, and for US healthcare we deploy on providers that sign a BAA.

| Several calls at once | Yes, for routing only | One call per person | Yes |
| Books, looks up and updates records | Rarely | Yes | Yes, through your integrations |
