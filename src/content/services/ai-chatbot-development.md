## Rule-based chatbot vs AI chatbot vs AI agent

| Compared on | Rule-based chatbot | AI chatbot | AI agent |
|---|---|---|---|
| How it works | Buttons, keywords and a fixed decision tree | Understands free text and answers from your content | Understands the request and takes actions in your systems |
| Handles unexpected wording | No | Yes | Yes |
| Answers from your documents | Only pre-written replies | Yes | Yes |
| Takes actions such as refunds or bookings | Only scripted ones | Through set integrations | Yes, with approval gates |
| Best for | Simple menus and fixed forms | FAQs, product help and internal knowledge | Support and operations work that ends in an action |
| Effort to build | Lowest | Moderate | Highest |
