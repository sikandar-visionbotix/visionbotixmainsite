## No-code MVP vs freelancer vs product studio

| Compared on | No-code MVP | Freelancer | Product studio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best for | Testing demand with a simple flow | A small, well-defined feature | A product you plan to grow and sell |
| Design, build, QA and launch | You fill the gaps | One person covers what they can | One team covers all of it |
| Billing, roles and multi-tenancy | Basic, within the platform | Depends on the person | Built in from the start |
| What happens when you scale | Often a rebuild | Depends on code quality | Grows by adding modules |
| Continuity if someone leaves | The platform stays | Risky | Team, documentation and a named lead |
| Who owns the code | The platform | Depends on the contract | You own all of it |
