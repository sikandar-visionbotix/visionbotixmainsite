## Who we are

This website, visionbotix.com, is operated by Visionbotix SMC-Private Limited ("Visionbotix", "we", "us" or "our"), an AI and software development studio based at 2nd Floor, Meridian Software Technology Park, Satellite Town, Murree Road, Rawalpindi, Pakistan.

We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. For any privacy question or request, email us at [info@visionbotix.com](mailto:info@visionbotix.com).

## What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit visionbotix.com or contact us through it, how we use it and what rights you have.

It does not cover:

- **Client project data.** When we build software for a client, any personal data we handle for that project is governed by the client contract and its data processing agreement (DPA), not by this policy.
- **Other websites and services**, including our own product sites and the third-party tools linked from this site. Each has its own privacy policy.

## What we collect and why

### Contact and quote form

When you use our contact or quote form, we collect:

- your name
- your work email address
- your company (optional)
- your country (optional)
- a description of what you need
- your budget range (optional)
- how you heard about us (optional)
- your time zone, so we can suggest call times
- the first page you opened on our site and the website that sent you there (for example a search engine or an AI assistant such as ChatGPT), so we know which pages and channels bring enquiries

Our cost estimator's "send the breakdown" form collects your name, work email, an optional note, your estimate and the same time zone and visit details.

We use this information to reply to you, prepare a quote and keep a record of our conversation. The form is processed by a serverless function on our hosting provider, delivered to our inbox by an email delivery provider (Resend) and may also be recorded in the spreadsheet or CRM we use to keep track of enquiries. To keep out spam, our forms may use Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks technical signals from your browser to tell people from automated programs.

We do not sell this information and we do not use it for unrelated marketing. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

### The "describe what you need" box

Our homepage has a box where you can describe your project to see which of our services are relevant. This runs entirely in your browser. The text you type is not sent to us or stored by us, unless you choose to carry it into the contact form and submit that form.

### The cost estimator

Our cost estimator works out a price range in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us. They are kept only in the page address, so you can bookmark or share the result, unless you choose to send us the breakdown or carry it into the contact form.

### Free tools

Our [AI visibility checker](/tools/ai-visibility-checker/) fetches the public web page you enter, plus that site's robots.txt, sitemap and llms.txt, and shows you a report. To do that, the address you enter is sent to our server. We keep the report in memory for up to 10 minutes so that a repeat check is quick, and our log records only the website's host name and its score, not your IP address. A Cloudflare Turnstile check, as on our forms, keeps automated abuse out, and a per-address limit on checks is enforced with a short-lived counter based on a one-way hash of your IP address. Nothing reaches our team unless you choose to send us the report with your name and email address, in which case it is handled like the contact form.

Our [AI readiness score](/tools/ai-readiness-score/) runs entirely in your browser: your answers are not sent to us or stored by us. The link to your results holds the answers in the web address itself, so anyone you share it with sees the same result. Nothing reaches our team unless you choose to send us your results with your name and email address, in which case they are handled like the contact form.

### Booking a call

If you book a call with us, the booking is handled by our scheduling provider, Cal.com. It collects the details you enter (such as your name, email address and chosen time) and shares them with us so we can hold the meeting. Your use of the scheduling tool is also governed by that provider's own privacy policy.

### WhatsApp

If you message us on WhatsApp, we receive your phone number, your profile name and the messages you send. We use them only to reply to you. WhatsApp is operated by Meta, and Meta's terms and privacy policy apply to your use of it.

### Analytics, only with your consent

With your permission, we use two analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site and to improve it:

- **Google Analytics 4**, which measures visits, pages viewed, approximate location, device type and how people arrive at the site. IP addresses are truncated by default in Google Analytics 4.
- **Microsoft Clarity**, which provides heatmaps and session replays that show how pages are scrolled and clicked. Text entered into form fields is masked, so it is not captured in replays.

These tools load **only after you click accept** in our consent banner. If you decline, neither of them loads and no analytics cookies are set. You can change your choice at any time using the **Cookie settings** link in the footer of every page.

### Strictly necessary browser storage

We store two small settings in your browser's local storage:

- your theme preference (light or dark)
- your cookie consent choice

These stay on your device, are not sent to us, and are needed for the site to work as you expect, so they do not require consent.

### Server logs

Our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically records technical information when your browser requests a page, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. These logs are kept by our host for security, abuse prevention and reliability.

### Live AI demos

Our live demos (at /demos/ and on some agent pages) and the "Ask our AI" concierge in the corner of every page let you chat with AI agents. The demo businesses and their data are fictional. Here is what happens to what you send:

- **What we process:** the messages you type or speak, your time zone (so the sample calendars show your dates), a small sandbox state that your browser sends back with each message, and your IP address for rate limits and the Cloudflare Turnstile human check.
- **The AI providers:** your messages are sent to **Groq** (United States) to generate the reply and to score them for prompt-injection attempts. When Groq is busy, the same message goes to one of our backup providers instead: **Cerebras** (United States), **Google's Gemini API** or **OpenRouter** (United States), which passes it to the model's host. Some of these are free services whose terms allow the provider to use what you send to improve their products, which is one more reason to use made-up details. We do not use your messages to train AI models.
- **Voice:** the receptionist demo uses your browser's own speech recognition. In Chrome, your audio is sent to Google to be transcribed; Safari may use Apple's service. We receive only the text. The agent's voice is produced by your browser.
- **Logs:** for each turn we log the demo name, your message and the reply (shortened to 300 characters), which tools ran, any guardrail warnings and the timing. These logs stay in our host's logs for no more than 30 days, to stop abuse and improve the demos. We do not use them for advertising.
- **The concierge:** if you ask it to have our team contact you, it shows exactly what it will send (your name, email, what you need and, if you gave them, budget and timeline, plus your messages in that chat). Nothing is sent until you press **Send**; after that it is handled like a contact form enquiry.

Please use made-up details in the demos: they are a sandbox. A filter removes card numbers and API keys before a message reaches the AI.

## Cookies and similar technologies

The table below lists the cookies and browser storage the site uses.

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Type | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Theme preference | Visionbotix (localStorage) | Remembers light or dark mode | Strictly necessary | Until you clear it |
| Consent choice | Visionbotix (localStorage) | Remembers your cookie choice | Strictly necessary | Until you clear it |
| Visit source | Visionbotix (sessionStorage) | Remembers how this visit started (the referring website and first page), for a form you choose to send and, if you accept analytics, for measurement | Functional | Until you close the tab |
| Offer shown | Visionbotix (localStorage) | Remembers that you have seen our "before you go" offer, so it is not shown again | Functional | Until you clear it |
| Enquiry sent | Visionbotix (sessionStorage) | Stops that offer appearing after you have sent us a form | Functional | Until you close the tab |
| Concierge chat | Visionbotix (sessionStorage) | Keeps your conversation with the "Ask our AI" concierge while you move between pages | Functional | Until you close the tab |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics 4 | Distinguishes visitors and sessions for usage statistics | Analytics, consent required | Up to 13 months |
| `_clck`, `_clsk` | Microsoft Clarity | Links page views into sessions for heatmaps and replays | Analytics, consent required | Up to 1 year |

We do not use advertising cookies.

## Legal bases for processing

If you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the following legal bases under the GDPR and UK GDPR:

- **Inquiries, form submissions, call bookings and messages:** our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, and taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
- **Live AI demos and the concierge:** our legitimate interest in providing the demo you asked to use and preventing its abuse; for a concierge request to contact you, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
- **Analytics:** your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through the Cookie settings link.
- **Strictly necessary storage and server logs:** our legitimate interest in running a secure, reliable website that remembers your basic settings.

## Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run the site and respond to you, and only as needed for that purpose:

- **Vercel**, our hosting provider, which serves the site, runs the contact form function and keeps server logs.
- **Resend**, our email delivery provider, which delivers form submissions to our inbox.
- **Cloudflare**, for its Turnstile spam check on our forms and live demos.
- **Groq**, which runs the AI models behind the live demos and the concierge, and, when it is busy, **Cerebras**, **Google** (Gemini API) or **OpenRouter** and the model host it routes to.
- **The spreadsheet or CRM service** we use to keep track of enquiries.
- **Cal.com**, our scheduling provider, if you book a call.
- **Google** and **Microsoft**, for analytics, only if you have given consent.
- **Meta**, if you choose to contact us on WhatsApp.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of our business, in which case this policy would continue to apply to your data.

We do not sell your personal data.

## International transfers

Our team is based in Pakistan, and our service providers are based in the United States and the European Union. This means your personal data may be processed in countries other than your own, which may have different data protection laws.

Where personal data is transferred out of the EU, EEA or UK, we rely on our providers' standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards recognized under applicable law.

## How long we keep data

- **Inquiries** (form submissions, emails, call bookings and messages): up to 3 years after our last contact with you. If you become a client, we keep relevant records for as long as the contract requires and as needed to meet legal and accounting record-keeping obligations.
- **Analytics data:** up to 13 months for Google Analytics 4 and up to 1 year for Microsoft Clarity.
- **Browser storage:** until you clear it from your browser.
- **Server logs:** for the period set by our hosting provider for security and reliability purposes.
- **Live demo logs:** no more than 30 days.

We delete or anonymize data when it is no longer needed.

## How we protect data

- All traffic to and from the site is encrypted with TLS.
- Access to inquiries and related records is limited to the people who need it, on a least-privilege basis.
- We work with established providers that maintain their own security programs.
- We are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) from the first call, before you share anything sensitive about your project.

## Your rights

### If you are in the EU, EEA or UK

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR, you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you
- **rectify** data that is inaccurate or incomplete
- **erase** your data
- **restrict** how we process your data
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interests
- **data portability**, meaning a copy of the data you gave us in a commonly used format
- **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting processing that took place before you withdrew it
- **complain to a supervisory authority**, such as the data protection authority in your country or, in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office

### If you are a California resident

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to request a copy, to ask us to correct or delete it, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that would give rise to a right to limit its use.

### If you are in Pakistan

We handle personal data in line with the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016 and other applicable Pakistani law.

### Everyone else

Wherever you are, you can ask us what we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. We will honor reasonable requests.

### How to exercise your rights

Email [info@visionbotix.com](mailto:info@visionbotix.com) with your request. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on it. We will respond within 30 days. If we need more time for a complex request, we will tell you why within that period.

To withdraw analytics consent, use the **Cookie settings** link in the footer. You can also clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings.

## Children

This site is meant for businesses and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
